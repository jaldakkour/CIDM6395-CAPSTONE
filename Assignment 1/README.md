#Assignment 1: Assessing Data Analytics

Data Analytics is a topic in the Master of Science in Computer Information Systems and Business Analytics (MS CISBA) degree program offers courses which focus on providing students with the skills and mindset needed to make data-driven, effective, and strategic business decisions. The courses that I took as a part of this section of the MS CISBA degree program include CIDM5310 Business Intelligence and Decision Support Systems (CIDM5310) and CIDM6308 Seminar in Data Analytics (CIDM6308). CIDM5310 has an emphasis on teaching students why analytic skills are vital to a business strategy, the data processing lifecycle, skills in python coding and Streamlit, as well as how artificial intelligence (AI) and large language models (LLMs) can be utilized in business workflows. CIDM6308 had an emphasis on teaching students the foundational knowledge of data analytics including data handling, visualization, supervised and unsupervised learning, statistical and machine learning models, as well as skills in using programs such as Altair Rapidminer, Tableau, SQL, and Microsoft Excel. Both courses helped me to develop strong skills and understanding in analytics processes and programs, however there were also areas that were left wanting.
